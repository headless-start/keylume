# Code of conduct

Keylume is made by people who like lighting up keyboards. Everyone is welcome to use it, ask
about it, report problems, design for it and contribute to it, whatever their experience,
background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect people's time: search before you ask, and say what you tried.
- Respect people's privacy: don't post anyone's personal details, and leave your own (names,
  emails, gamer tags, game account IDs) out of logs and screenshots you share.
- Designs are our own: don't submit other people's work, or franchise, character or brand
  names ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## What isn't welcome

Harassment of any kind; insults, slurs and personal attacks; sexual content or attention;
threats; trolling; and publishing anyone's private information. This holds in the
repository, its issues and discussions, and anywhere you speak for the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** form (it's private, and reaches only the maintainers): say it's about conduct,
and what happened, where and when. We read every report, keep it confidential, and reply.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, code and issues that break these rules, and may
warn, or ban for a time or for good, anyone who does. We'll say why.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
