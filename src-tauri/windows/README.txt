Keylume
=======

Lighting and keys for your RGB keyboard, entirely on this PC.

- Tens of thousands of designs: games, heroes, myths, nature, space, flags,
  colours and effects. Open one to see all of its looks, then click to use it.
- Paint your own per-key designs, tune the keyboard's animations, or run live
  effects that follow your music, a timer or your screen.
- Design packs: add new collections with one file.
- Remap keys, record macros, set the side light and the keyboard's own settings.
- True colours: colours adjusted for the LEDs, so the keys come closer to
  what you see in the app.

Local only
----------
Keylume never connects to the internet. There is no account, no cloud, no
telemetry and no update check. Your profiles and settings stay in your user
folder.

Your keyboard
-------------
Keylume recognises your keyboard when you plug it in and adapts to it: its shape,
its lights and what it can do. Only one program can talk to the keyboard at a
time: if the maker's own app starts, Keylume pauses and resumes by itself.
