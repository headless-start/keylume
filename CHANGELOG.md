## 1.0.0

The first public release.

- **Home** shows your keyboard as it looks, lit with what's really on its keys, with the
  name of the look and whether it's connected.
- **Library**: about 1,400 designs in more than 31,000 looks, drawn for the connected
  keyboard's shape. Games (on each game's default keys, lit by role), comics, themes,
  colours, flags of the world and effects. Search by word or colour, favourites, and
  Surprise me.
- **Create**: paint keys, tune the keyboard's own animations, build live effects (music,
  screen colour, timers) and spell words across the keys.
- **Keys**: remap any key on the three onboard profiles and the Fn layer, and record macros.
  On 60 and 65 % keyboards the standard Fn layer puts F1 … F12 on Fn + the number row.
  Every key change is read back from the keyboard before it counts as done.
- **Side light**, and the keyboard's own settings (polling rate, debounce, sleep, Windows
  key lock, WASD swap, onboard profile), with backup and restore of everything on the
  keyboard.
- **Design packs**: `.keylumepack` files with themes and hand-made designs, signed with
  Ed25519. Two come with the app (Solar Terms, Life's Moments); make your own from your
  designs, to give away or sell.
- **True colours**: every colour is adjusted for LEDs, so the keys come closer to the screen.
- **Keyboards**: the Epomaker TK68 (verified); HID LampArray keyboards (experimental).
  Keyboards are described as data, and Keylume writes only to a keyboard it has identified
  exactly.
- **Local only**: no account, no cloud, no telemetry, no network connections.
- Windows installer; Linux `.deb` and AppImage.
